A logo is the first taste.

Before anyone reads your menu, they read your sign. I'm Kamalov Elyor, and I design logos for one kind of place only: the ones where people eat, drink and linger.

Every mark is drawn to look right from across the street and on the lid of a paper cup. If it can't be sketched from memory on a napkin, it isn't finished.

What you get

  • A mark that is only yours

    Your name, drawn so nobody will mistake it for the place next door.

  • Colors and type that taste right

    A palette that holds up under warm cafe light, on paper and on a phone screen.

  • Versions for every surface

    Full color, one color and stacked layouts for stamps, signs and tiny social icons.

  • Ready for real life

    Ideas for menus, cups, boxes and doors, so the logo works the day you open.

Why it pays off

  • Seen before tasted

    Guests decide in seconds. Your logo is the first thing they judge, long before the food.

  • Simple beats clever

    The marks people remember are the ones they can draw. Fewer shapes, stronger memory.

  • Built to be repeated

    A good logo survives being stamped, stitched, steamed on a window and shrunk to a thumbnail.

How a project works

  1. Brief

    We talk through your menu, location, guests and the mood you want.

  2. Concepts

    You receive three distinct directions with colors and type.

  3. Refine

    Two rounds of revisions on the direction you choose.

  4. Delivery

    Vector files, full color, one color, stacked and horizontal layouts.
